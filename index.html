Our Story • How We Started

Honest, Personalised Guidance for Every Student's Global Dream

Godwit International was born from a simple but powerful belief — that every student deserves honest, personalised guidance when navigating the complex world of overseas education. Founded by a team of experienced education professionals in Vadodara, we set out to bridge the gap between aspiring students and their dream universities abroad.

Located on Sama-Savli Road, opposite Urmi School in New Sama, our office has become a trusted destination for hundreds of families across Gujarat. From our first consultation to our most recent visa approval, we have remained committed to transparency, personalisation, and genuine care for every student's future.

Today, Godwit International is recognised as one of Vadodara's premier study abroad consultancies — with a 5.0 Google rating, 500+ successful student placements, and a growing network spanning 10+ countries.

Our 3-Stage Assessment-First Methodology

Every student is added to a dedicated private mentor group for continuous homework review & feedback.

Free Books • Audio Notes • Online Portal • Reading Library
Stage 01 • Foundation Stage

Pure Language & Grammar

Focus is purely on core language fundamentals. No mock tests are conducted until grammar, vocabulary, and sentence structure are rock-solid.

Stage 02 • Active Module Stage

2–3 Daily Module Tests

Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking are taught and tested independently until you master each individual module.

Stage 03 • Exam-Ready Stage

Full-Length Mock Simulations

2 full-length mock tests per week (ramping up to 2 per day with immediate 1-on-1 feedback right before your exam date).
